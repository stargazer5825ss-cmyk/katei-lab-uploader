Katei Lab Uploader

A private upload tool for the YouTube channel “仮定ラボ (Katei Lab)”.

This tool uses YouTube API Services

What it is

Katei Lab Uploader is a private command-line tool that runs only on the channel owner's computer. It uploads short educational videos (YouTube Shorts) that the owner has made and reviewed to the owner's own YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@仮定ラボ-note. It is not offered to anyone else.

How it works

  1. The owner renders a video on their own computer.
  2. The owner reviews the video, its title, and its description, and approves them.
  3. The tool uploads the approved video to the owner's channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert) as a private video.
  4. The owner checks the video and publishes it in YouTube Studio.
  5. After the video is public, the tool posts a comment that the owner wrote and approved on that video. The owner pins it in YouTube.

The tool signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 and requests two scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (to upload the owner's videos) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl (only to check whether the owner's own uploaded video is public and to post the owner's comment on it).

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect, or store data about other YouTube users or channels.
  • It does not download, modify, or redistribute anyone else's content.
  • It has no server, no user accounts, no cookies, and no analytics.
